# Using NexRoad

NexRoad simulates roadside assistance. It does not dispatch real responders or move money. [Open NexRoad](https://nexroad.vercel.app)

## Try the demo

On the sign-in page, enter the assigned email and password. Use the Copy button next to either field to copy its current value. Supabase determines which full dashboard opens. These accounts use the same hosted NexRoad project and can change data according to their normal permissions. Use the admin account only with authorized operators.

The public [sample preview](https://nexroad.vercel.app/demo/customer) is also available by direct link and stays in the browser.

## Customer

1. Register a customer account and sign in. Confirm your email if prompted.
2. Save a vehicle if you want to associate it with the request. Open **Request assistance**, choose fuel, mechanical assistance or towing, and set the pickup by GPS, map or manual entry.
3. Fill in the service details, choose the simulated payment method and select **Review price**. Confirm the displayed INR quote to send the request.
4. Follow status and technician assignment from **Active requests**. You can cancel before acceptance. Once assigned, use booking chat and view location when available.
5. Review and confirm the service report. A mechanical report can offer a separately quoted follow-up tow. Record simulated payment when prompted and download the receipt.
6. Find completed requests in history and leave feedback or submit a complaint where available.

## Technician

1. Apply for a technician account. While pending, use the application screen to check status. An administrator must approve the application.
2. Configure active services and rates, go online and enable request acceptance. Share your location when you are ready to receive nearby offers.
3. Accept an eligible offer before it expires, or decline it. Finish your current active job before accepting another.
4. Open the assigned job, start travel, mark arrival and start service. Use booking chat for customer coordination.
5. Submit the service report with the delivered fuel quantity, mechanical outcome or towing drop-off as required. The customer confirms completion. Confirmed simulated payments appear in earnings.

## Administrator

1. Sign in using an authorized admin account. Use the applications section to review and approve eligible technicians.
2. Monitor bookings, simulated payments and technicians who are sharing location. Open complaints to record a resolution and review admin activity afterward.
3. Manage service defaults, fuel prices and platform settings. Rate changes apply to new quotes. Existing bookings retain their accepted prices.
4. Suspend or reactivate accounts when appropriate. Review audit history for the administrative action.
